\begin{itemize}

\item The short timeframe for Area 2W surveys continues to be a
challenge but the Port Louis area was fully assessed again in
2026.

\item Samples were collected in Port Louis, Rennell Sound, and Inskip.

\item Surface surveys were completed on spawns in Rennell Sound and Dawson Harbour.
Weather prevented a surface assessment of the Inskip area.

\item Spawning in the Port Louis area was limited to the head of Otard Bay.
Spawn timing, although later than the previous two years,
was close to the average for years on record.

\end{itemize}
