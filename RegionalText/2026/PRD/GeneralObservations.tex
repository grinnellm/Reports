\subsection{Areas 3 and 4 (Big Bay)}

\begin{itemize}

\item Nothing unusual this year in terms of spawning;
the season followed a typical pattern.
Juvenile component holding and spawning in the North.

\item Light layers recorded in the southern and northern
portions of the assessment area.

\item Average of 4 layers observed around the Tree Bluff
area leading into Big Bay along the southern shore.

\item Average of 6 layers observed in the North
throughout Stumaun Bay and Rush Brook Passage.

\end{itemize}

\subsection{Area 5 (Kitkatla)}

\begin{itemize}

\item Kitkatla had strong spawn abundance and
spawn started later than in previous years.

\end{itemize}
