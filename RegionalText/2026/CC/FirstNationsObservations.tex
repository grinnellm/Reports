\subsection{Heiltsuk}

Guardian watchmen had difficulty catching herring samples, and
comments that there were fewer herring and fewer intertidal spawns. 

Herring went into Spiller channel but
didn't stay long and migrated out towards Seaforth and Dundavin.
Seine test boat followed the herring, which were travelling at 4 to 6 knots
from Spiller to Dundavin through Rait narrows.
The captain thought that the fish from Spiller
was the same fish in Styker, Thompson, and Waskesiu.

The inlets were 2 to 3 degrees cooler than outer waters
(e.g., Spiller channel was cooler than Seaforth channel), and
outer areas such as Stryker, Thompson, and Waskesiu were warmer.
Fish moved westward, towards warmer water.

Stock was spread out thinner,
estimate of 12k tons from gillnet vessels and
14k to 17k from the herring test boat. 

Fish in Spiller seemed small, and
outside fish in Stryker and Thompson were bigger.
Observations of clearwater spawn in Spiller lagoon (Tate) 
Area 8: Big spawn from Kwakume down to Illahe.
Stewart inlet: good spawn, estimate of 2k to 3k tons.

Better spawning out in Stryker, Thompson, and Waskesue.

Very little herring spawn for FSC, just a few households (6) got herring roe.

Water was a different colour, more brownish.
