\begin{itemize}

\item Active spawn timing was widespread, with
active spawn reported or observed between January \nth{9} and April \nth{27}.

\item Vessel and shore-based dive surveys were performed
on all observed spawns except for
early Hesquiat Harbour, Bartlett Island, Whaler Islet, outside of Nootka Island,
Quatsino Sound, late Ucluelet Harbour, and late Nootka Sound.

\item Increased FSC harvest (anecdotally) and
five Nations right-based sale fishery for SOK opened for the first time.

\end{itemize}
