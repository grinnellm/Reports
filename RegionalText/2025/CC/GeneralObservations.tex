\begin{itemize}

\item Generally good weather resulted in minimal interference with the
assessment program.

\item First spawn reported on March \nth{26} in Area 6 (Kitasu Bay).

\item Sea surface temperatures through March and the start of
April were unusually cold, averaging 5$^\circ\text{C}$.

\item Early indications of spawning activity were not favourable.
Spawning conditions improved significantly as the season progressed.

\item In Area 7, FSC product was generally above average.
Notable spawn layers observed in lower Spiller Channel.

\end{itemize}
