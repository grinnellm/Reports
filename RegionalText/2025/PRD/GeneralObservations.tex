\subsection{Big Bay}

\begin{itemize}

\item Earliest spawn on record but exact timing is unknown
(first satellite image on Mar \nth{12}),
possibly due to a mild winter.

\item Spawns exhibited a spot-spawn pattern throughout the season.

\item Second consecutive year of historical two-part spawn (early and late),
with more spawn in the inlet.

\end{itemize}

\subsection{Kitkatla}

\begin{itemize}

\item First active spawn reported by Gitxaala on March \nth{13} which is
further South than ever before.

\item Overall, multiple spawns with varied timing.
Later spawns were predominantly spot spawns.

\item Test sets contained mostly adult fish; few juveniles.

\end{itemize}
